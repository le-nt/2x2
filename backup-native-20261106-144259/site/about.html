How it works

The name is the first thing you ever built with: a 2×2 brick. Nothing here is a video stream or a remake. When you press Play, your browser downloads:

  • BoxedWine, an x86 and Linux emulator compiled to WebAssembly, with WebGL graphics.
  • A small Linux system with Wine, the Windows compatibility layer.
  • The mid-2008 ROBLOX client from Novetus, and the place you picked.

The emulator boots Wine and runs the original, unmodified client on your own computer, inside the tab. Its Direct3D 9 graphics are translated to WebGL, and a small Lua script spawns your character, the same idea as Novetus' solo mode.

It's emulation all the way down, so expect it to be slower than the real thing and to take a minute or two to boot the first time.
