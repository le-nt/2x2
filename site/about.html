How it works

2x2 brings classic places into your browser with Project Pizda’s native WebAssembly client.

Your website account and selected character are used when you join a game. The entrance key protects the website and game connections; use Lock website to require it again.

Games become available after their original map files and the native game client are installed. Missing game files are reported by the launcher.
